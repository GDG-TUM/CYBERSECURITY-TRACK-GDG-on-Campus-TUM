## Light weeks
Exam weeks and breaks are marked as light weeks, with only a Cyber Clinic or a short take-home mission. **[TO CONFIRM]** exact academic calendar dates.

The order of labs within weeks 5 to 8 is set by the Labs/CTF Coordinator. **[TO CONFIRM]**

## Principles
- **Legal and ethical, always**
- **Learn by doing:** labs completed, not just attendance
- **Defence and offence together:** learn how attacks work to build better defences
- **Write it up:** explaining something is the best way to learn it
- **Help each other:** today's beginner is tomorrow's mentor
