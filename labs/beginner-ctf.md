# Beginner CTF

**Stage:** Practice (Practitioner) · **When:** Week 10 · **Time:** 2–3 hours

**Goal:** A friendly capture-the-flag in teams. Completing it is part of earning the Practitioner badge.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Practise only on systems built for it. See the [rules of engagement](../ETHICS-AND-RULES.md).

## What you need
A team of 3 to 4, a browser, and a free account on the chosen platform (for example picoCTF) or the club-hosted challenges.

## What you will be able to do
- Understand how a CTF works
- Work as a team and share findings
- Solve beginner challenges in categories such as general skills, web, forensics and cryptography
- Record your score and write up one challenge

## Steps
**Format:** jeopardy-style challenges worth points. Teams pick challenges, solve them and submit flags.

**Team roles:** a note-taker, a researcher, a driver at the keyboard, and a reviewer. Rotate every challenge.

**Good habits**
- Read the challenge carefully and keep notes as you go
- Share what you try and what you learn
- Stay inside the competition's rules and scope
- Never attack the competition infrastructure itself

## ✅ Lab checkpoints
Log these with the Labs/CTF Coordinator. Labs completed, not attendance, count towards your badge.
- [ ] Team formed and roles assigned
- [ ] At least three challenges attempted
- [ ] Score recorded in the CTF log
- [ ] One challenge written up

## Safety
Beginner and stretch challenges are tiered so mixed skill levels can all take part. Scores feed the Practitioner badge.
