# 🛟 Risks and Fallbacks

| Risk | Fallback |
|------|----------|
| Low turnout | Shift to recorded labs and Cyber Clinics; promote through other GDG tracks and class groups |
| Coordinator drops out | Co-Lead covers; recruit a replacement from the Explorer cohort |
| Web track unavailable | Run the project internally as a *Build It. Break It. Secure It.* squad |
| No lab access or poor campus internet | Use local VMs and free tiers of TryHackMe or Hack The Box; offline-capable labs |
| Exam periods and breaks | Light weeks: Cyber Clinic or a short take-home mission only |
| Mixed skill levels in one room | Tiered tasks in each lab; advanced members get stretch challenges |
