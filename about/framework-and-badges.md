# 🛤️ Framework and Badges

The track uses **one pathway**. Levels are badges members earn along it, not separate programmes.

**Discover → Foundations → Practice → Build → Mentor**

| Stage | Who it serves | Badge / outcome | Page |
|-------|---------------|-----------------|------|
| Discover | Non-tech and curious students | **Explorer:** understands common threats and safe digital habits | [Stage 1](../learning-path/01-discover.md) |
| Foundations | Beginners | **Learner:** completes networking, Linux and web fundamentals labs | [Stage 2](../learning-path/02-foundations.md) |
| Practice | Learners ready for hands-on work | **Practitioner:** completes guided investigations and a beginner CTF | [Stage 3](../learning-path/03-practice.md) |
| Build | Practitioners (semester two focus) | **Builder:** contributes to a security-focused project | [Stage 4](../learning-path/04-build.md) |
| Mentor | Builders (semester two focus) | **Mentor:** supports beginners and helps deliver sessions | [Stage 5](../learning-path/05-mentor.md) |

## What runs in semester one
- **Explorer and Learner** run in full
- **Practitioner** is delivered as a single CTF and investigation block
- **One cross-track project (Cyber × Web)** represents the Build stage
- **Mentor** is piloted by shortlisting candidates only

## How badges are earned
Badges are measured by **work completed, not attendance**: labs finished, challenges solved and write-ups published, tracked in the lab checkpoint log and lab platform records. The exact checkpoints for each badge are published by the Labs/CTF Coordinator. **[TO CONFIRM]**

## Why a single framework?
- **Inclusive:** welcomes non-tech students, complete beginners and learners with some experience
- **Progressive:** each stage builds on the last
- **Practical:** labs, challenges and simulations, not theory alone
- **Career-oriented:** members leave with project evidence, a GitHub trail and clearer career direction
- **Sustainable:** strong learners become mentors, so the track continues
