# 👥 Team and Roles

The Lead **coordinates the ecosystem** rather than teaching every topic. Semester one runs with a **core team of four**. More coordinator roles are added in semester two as members prove reliable.

| Role | Responsibility |
|------|----------------|
| **Cybersecurity Lead** | Vision, roadmap, coordination, partnerships, progress tracking, final report |
| **Co-Lead** | Supports delivery, follows up on action items, coordinates sessions |
| **Labs/CTF Coordinator** | Lab environments, challenges and assessment |
| **Community/Mentorship Coordinator** | Beginner support, attendance, engagement, mentor shortlisting |
| **Defensive/SOC Coordinator** | Log analysis, investigations and blue-team activities |

**Semester two additions:** Offensive Security, Cloud Security, AI Security and Mobile Security coordinators.
