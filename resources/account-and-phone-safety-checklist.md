# 📱 Account and Phone Safety Checklist

Work through this for **your own** accounts and devices. It supports the Discover stage.

## Passwords
- [ ] Every important account has its own strong password or passphrase
- [ ] I use a password manager
- [ ] My email account is the most protected one, because it can reset everything else

## Two-factor authentication
- [ ] Turned on for email, GitHub and social media
- [ ] Backup codes saved somewhere safe and offline
- [ ] I prefer an authenticator app over SMS where I can

## Phishing and scams
- [ ] I never share one-time codes or PINs, even with someone claiming to be support
- [ ] I check the sender and link before tapping, especially on urgent messages
- [ ] I am careful with messages about winnings, jobs, fees, reversals or "wrong transfer" requests
- [ ] I confirm requests for money by calling the person on a number I already know

## Phone
- [ ] Screen lock is on, with a PIN or biometric that is hard to guess
- [ ] Software and apps are up to date, with automatic updates on
- [ ] I only install apps from the official store, and review the permissions they ask for
- [ ] Find-my-device and remote lock are turned on
- [ ] My SIM has a PIN and I know how to report a lost or stolen SIM to my network

## Mobile money
- [ ] Mobile money PIN is not shared and not the same as my phone PIN
- [ ] I know how to report a fraudulent transaction to my provider quickly

## Public wifi and sharing
- [ ] I avoid sensitive logins on public wifi, or use mobile data instead
- [ ] I think before posting personal details, ID photos or location

## If something goes wrong
1. Change the password of the affected account, starting with email
2. Turn on two-factor authentication
3. Sign out of all other devices
4. Tell your mobile network or bank if money or a SIM is involved
5. Tell someone you trust, and the Track Lead if it relates to the club
