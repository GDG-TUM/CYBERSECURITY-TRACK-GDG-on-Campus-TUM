# Cybersecurity on Your CV

**Format:** Career paths, portfolios, GitHub, certifications, internships

**Stage:** Discover (Explorer) · **When:** Weeks 3–4 (exact week and day **[TO CONFIRM]**)

**Goal:** See what cybersecurity careers look like and how to start building evidence now.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Practise only on systems built for it. See the [rules of engagement](../ETHICS-AND-RULES.md).

## What you will be able to do
- Name common entry roles and what they do
- Explain entry-level expectations honestly
- Build a small portfolio plan with GitHub write-ups
- Know which certifications and internships are worth considering

## How it runs
1. Careers map (20 min)
2. What employers look for at entry level (20 min)
3. Portfolio and GitHub workshop (30 min)
4. Q&A (10 min)
