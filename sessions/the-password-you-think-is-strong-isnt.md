# The Password You Think Is Strong… Isn't

**Format:** Password attacks, MFA and account takeover demonstrated safely

**Stage:** Discover (Explorer) · **When:** Discover block (week to be confirmed) (exact week and day **[TO CONFIRM]**)

**Goal:** Understand how accounts get taken over, and why long passphrases, unique passwords and multi-factor authentication work.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Practise only on systems built for it. See the [rules of engagement](../ETHICS-AND-RULES.md).

## What you will be able to do
- Explain why reuse and short passwords are risky
- Describe at a high level how attackers get into accounts
- Compare MFA options and their strengths
- Check whether your email appears in a known breach

## How it runs
1. How accounts are taken over, in concept (20 min)
2. Facilitator demo (15 min)
3. Set up MFA and a password manager (25 min)
4. Q&A (10 min)

## Safety and set-up notes
**Demonstrations use fictional accounts and sample data only.** No real credential lists, no real accounts, no step-by-step attack recipes. The point is understanding, not capability.

## 🎯 Take-home Cyber Mission
Move your five most important accounts to unique passwords and MFA.
