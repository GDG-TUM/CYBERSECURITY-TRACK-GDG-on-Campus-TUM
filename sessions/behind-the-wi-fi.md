# Behind the Wi-Fi

**Format:** What happens after you connect: packets, DNS, IPs

**Stage:** Foundations (Learner) · **When:** Weeks 5–8 (exact week and day **[TO CONFIRM]**)

**Goal:** Understand what happens between connecting to wifi and loading a page.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Practise only on systems built for it. See the [rules of engagement](../ETHICS-AND-RULES.md).

## What you will be able to do
- Explain IP and MAC addresses, DNS and DHCP
- Tell TCP and UDP apart and explain ports
- Describe how HTTP differs from HTTPS
- Read a simple view of your own network traffic

## How it runs
1. From the router to the page, step by step (25 min)
2. Look at your own connection details (20 min)
3. Intro to packet captures on a sample file (30 min)
4. Q&A (15 min)

## Safety and set-up notes
Only look at **your own** traffic or the sample capture provided by the team. Never capture traffic on a network you do not own.
