# Your Phone Is a Crime Scene

**Format:** Mobile privacy, permissions and what a device reveals

**Stage:** Discover (Explorer) · **When:** Weeks 3–4 (exact week and day **[TO CONFIRM]**)

**Goal:** See what your own phone reveals about you, and tighten it up.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Practise only on systems built for it. See the [rules of engagement](../ETHICS-AND-RULES.md).

## What you will be able to do
- Review app permissions, location history and advertising settings
- Explain what an investigator, or a thief, could learn from an unlocked phone
- Set up a strong screen lock, updates and find-my-device
- Know how to protect mobile money and your SIM

## How it runs
1. Why a phone is the richest target (10 min)
2. Guided audit of your own phone (30 min)
3. Group discussion: what surprised you? (15 min)
4. Action plan (5 min)

## Safety and set-up notes
Everyone audits **their own phone only**. Nobody looks at anyone else's device.

Finish the [account and phone safety checklist](../resources/account-and-phone-safety-checklist.md) and help one person do the same.
