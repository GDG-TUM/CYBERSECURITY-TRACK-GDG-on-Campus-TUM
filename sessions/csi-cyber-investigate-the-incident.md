# CSI: Cyber — Investigate the Incident

**Format:** Teams use logs and clues to reconstruct a fictional breach

**Stage:** Practice (Practitioner) · **When:** Weeks 9–10 (exact week and day **[TO CONFIRM]**)

**Goal:** Work like a defender: gather evidence, build a timeline and tell the story of an incident.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Practise only on systems built for it. See the [rules of engagement](../ETHICS-AND-RULES.md).

## What you will be able to do
- Read logs and spot unusual activity
- Build a timeline from several clues
- Identify what was affected and how it likely started
- Write a short incident summary and recommend fixes

## How it runs
1. Briefing: you are the response team (10 min)
2. Team investigation with the fictional case file (45 min)
3. Present your timeline (20 min)
4. Debrief: what would have stopped it? (15 min)

## Safety and set-up notes
The case is **fictional**, with made-up users and documentation-range addresses. The Defensive/SOC Coordinator prepares the case file.

## 🎯 Take-home Cyber Mission
Write a one-page incident summary and add it to your write-ups.
