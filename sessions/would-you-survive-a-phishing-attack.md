# Would You Survive a Phishing Attack?

**Format:** Interactive phishing detective challenge using realistic messages

**Stage:** Discover (Explorer) · **When:** Weeks 3–4 (exact week and day **[TO CONFIRM]**)

**Goal:** Learn to spot phishing and social engineering, including AI-written scams, and know what to do if you click.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Practise only on systems built for it. See the [rules of engagement](../ETHICS-AND-RULES.md).

## What you will be able to do
- Name the common red flags: sender, urgency, links, attachments, requests for codes or money
- Explain social engineering in plain language
- Describe what changes when AI writes the scam
- Know the first steps after clicking something suspicious

## How it runs
1. Welcome and why attackers target people (10 min)
2. Team challenge: sort a set of messages into real and phishing (25 min)
3. Debrief: what gave them away? (15 min)
4. What to do if you click (10 min)

## Safety and set-up notes
The facilitators prepare the message set in advance. **Use fictional brands, fictional people and made-up links only**, so no real organisation or person is impersonated.

## 🎯 Take-home Cyber Mission
Take the [phishing red flags guide](../resources/phishing-red-flags.md) home and test yourself on messages you receive this week.
