# ❓ FAQ

### I have no tech background. Can I join?
Yes. The **Discover** stage is built for non-tech and curious students. Nobody is thrown into advanced tools.

### Is this about hacking?
It is about understanding how attacks work so you can defend against them, and practising in **legal labs**. We attack only systems built for the purpose.

### Do I need a powerful laptop?
No. Most beginner labs run in the browser, and labs also run offline in local VMs.

### Is it free?
Yes. We use free tiers of TryHackMe and Hack The Box where possible. Budget for any paid tools is still to be confirmed.

### What are the badges?
**Explorer, Learner, Practitioner, Builder and Mentor.** They are earned along one pathway. See [framework and badges](about/framework-and-badges.md).

### What if I miss a session?
The labs and mission pages are in this repo, and [Cyber Clinics](engagement/README.md) are open sessions for questions and catching up.

### I found a weakness in a real website or app. What now?
Stop. Do not explore further, do not share it publicly, and tell the Track Lead. We will help you report it responsibly.

### Can I practise on my friend's phone, my school wifi or a real website?
No. Only on systems built for it, or that you own, or that you have written permission to test. See the [rules](ETHICS-AND-RULES.md).

### How can I help run the track?
Open call for the core team happens in weeks 1 and 2. See [team and roles](about/team-and-roles.md). Strong learners can become mentors in semester two.

### I accidentally committed a password or key. What now?
Tell a maintainer **immediately**. The secret must be revoked and replaced, since deleting the commit is not enough.

### How do I contribute?
Start by [adding your profile](members/README.md), then read the org [CONTRIBUTING guide](https://github.com/GDG-TUM/.github/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
