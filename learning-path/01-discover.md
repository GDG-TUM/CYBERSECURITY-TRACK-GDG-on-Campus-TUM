# Stage 1: Discover

**Badge:** Explorer · **Who it serves:** Non-tech and curious students · **When:** Weeks 3–4

**Goal:** Understand common threats and build safe digital habits.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Practise only on systems built for it. See the [rules of engagement](../ETHICS-AND-RULES.md).

## What you will learn
- Cybersecurity for everyone
- The CIA triad: confidentiality, integrity, availability
- Common threats, phishing and social engineering
- Privacy and your digital footprint
- Mobile safety
- AI-powered scams
- Cybersecurity careers

## Sessions and labs
- [Would You Survive a Phishing Attack?](../sessions/would-you-survive-a-phishing-attack.md)
- [Your Phone Is a Crime Scene](../sessions/your-phone-is-a-crime-scene.md)
- [The Password You Think Is Strong… Isn't](../sessions/the-password-you-think-is-strong-isnt.md)
- [Cybersecurity on Your CV](../sessions/cybersecurity-on-your-cv.md)

## Hands-on
1. Secure your email and phone using the [checklist](../resources/account-and-phone-safety-checklist.md).
2. Practise with the [phishing red flags guide](../resources/phishing-red-flags.md).
3. Try the first [Cyber Mission](../engagement/README.md).

## ✅ Checkpoint
- Can I explain confidentiality, integrity and availability with everyday examples?
- Can I spot the red flags in a suspicious message?
- Is my own email account protected with two-factor authentication?

If you can answer these, move to the next stage. If not, revisit the topics or ask in the channel.
