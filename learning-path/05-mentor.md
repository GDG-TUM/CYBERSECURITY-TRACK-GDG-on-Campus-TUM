# Stage 5: Mentor

**Badge:** Mentor · **Who it serves:** Builders (semester two focus) · **When:** Pilot in weeks 11–12

**Goal:** Support beginners and help deliver sessions.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Practise only on systems built for it. See the [rules of engagement](../ETHICS-AND-RULES.md).

## What you will learn
- Explaining ideas clearly and kindly
- Supporting beginners in labs
- Helping run sessions and Cyber Clinics

## Sessions and labs
- [Team and roles](../about/team-and-roles.md)
- [Missions, clinics and challenges](../engagement/README.md)

## Hands-on
1. In semester one, **candidates are shortlisted only** (target: 3 members) during weeks 11 and 12.
2. The structured mentor programme is planned for semester two.

## ✅ Checkpoint
- Can I explain a concept so a complete beginner understands?
- Do I help others without doing the work for them?
- Do I follow and model the rules of engagement?

If you can answer these, move to the next stage. If not, revisit the topics or ask in the channel.
