# 🎯 Missions, Clinics and Challenges

Ways to keep learning between sessions.

## Cyber Missions
Small tasks between sessions, such as "turn on MFA for your email" or "spot three red flags in messages you get this week". Each session page ends with a take-home mission.

## Progress badges
**Explorer, Learner and Practitioner** in semester one. See [framework and badges](../about/framework-and-badges.md).

## Cyber Clinics
Open sessions for questions and problems. They also serve as the activity in **light weeks** (exam periods and breaks). Bring a lab you are stuck on, or a question about security at home.

## Monthly challenge
One **beginner** and one **advanced** challenge each month, so mixed skill levels both have something to try.

## Project squads
Mixed-skill teams on the [Cyber × Web project](../projects/cyber-x-web/README.md).

## Portfolio checkpoints
GitHub, documentation and write-ups are built into the learning process. See [projects](../projects/README.md).

## Guest practitioner
At least one session with a SOC analyst or other security practitioner. **[TO CONFIRM]** speaker and budget.

## Want to help?
Mentor candidates are shortlisted in weeks 11 and 12. See [team and roles](../about/team-and-roles.md).
